Landscape Design-Build RFP

Ava, Missouri | 12-home residential development

Bright Home Investments is seeking a landscape design-build team to complete the landscape design for Astoria Ln., install it, and provide first-season establishment care. The work includes the 12 home lots and the separate shared green-space areas shown in the RFP exhibits.

Project focus

  • No conventional turf lawns; use no-mow or low-mow groundcover and planting strategies.
  • Native Missouri and well-adapted plants, including trees, shrubs, perennials, groundcovers, and edible plants.
  • Defined shared green spaces including the Walking Meadow, Nature/Sensory Garden, E. Grand screening, and stormwater-compatible planting where appropriate.
  • Accessible routes and usable gathering/rest areas without losing the natural character of the landscape.
  • First growing season care, followed by handoff to Bright Grounds for long-term maintenance.
