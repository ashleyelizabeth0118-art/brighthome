The Neighborhood

Built right here, on the land.

Astoria Ln is a new neighborhood taking shape on Shull St., inside the Ava city limits — on city water, sewer, and electric, in the Ava R-I school district.

Every home is built on-site using the Xtrata SABS panelized building system. The panels go up fast and fit tight, which means a stronger, better-insulated home and a more predictable build — not a factory box dropped on a lot, but a real home built on its own foundation.
