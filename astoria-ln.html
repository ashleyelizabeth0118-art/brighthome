Ava, Missouri

The Treasure of the Ozarks.

Ava is the county seat of Douglas County, in the southern Missouri Ozarks — close-knit, rural, and built around people who've lived and worked here for generations. The town sits near the Mark Twain National Forest, about 45 minutes from Springfield and Branson. Local schools, a library, a hospital, and a town square anchor daily life here — the kind of place where neighbors know each other by name.

Astoria Ln is a new neighborhood taking shape inside the Ava city limits — on city water, sewer, and electric, in the Ava R-I school district.
